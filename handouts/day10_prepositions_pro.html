Unit 02. 시점 vs 기간 및 논리 전치사

01. 시점(Point) vs 기간(Period) 전치사

시점 전치사 (+특정 날/시각) 기간 전치사 (+숫자/기간명사)
  • By: ~까지 (완료의 의미)
  • Until: ~까지 (상태의 지속)
  • Since: ~이래로 (현재완료와 결합)
  • From: ~부터
  • For: ~동안 (숫자 기간)
  • During: ~동안 (특정 행사/사건)
  • Within: ~이내에
  • Throughout: ~내내, ~도처에
1초 구분: By vs Until

By (완료): 제출하다(submit), 끝내다(finish), 배달하다(deliver)와 짝꿍
Until (지속): 머무르다(stay), 열려있다(remain open), 계속되다(continue)와 짝꿍

02. 이유, 양보, 목적의 전치사

  • 이유 (~때문에): because of, due to, owing to, on account of
  • 양보 (~에도 불구하고): despite, in spite of, notwithstanding, with all
  • 목적 (~을 위하여): for (for your convenience, for future use)

Unit 03. 위치·방향 및 필수 테마 전치사

01. 숫자·수치 수식 전치사 (Over/Under/Above/Below)

  • Above / Over: ~보다 위에 / 이상인
  • Below / Under: ~보다 아래에 / 이하인
  • Between / Among: (둘) / (셋 이상) 사이에
  • Beyond: ~를 넘어서 / ~이 불가능한
  • Against: ~에 반대하여 / ~에 기대어
  • Beside / Next to: ~옆에

02. "~에 관하여" 전치사 패밀리

토익 RC에서 가장 정답률이 높은 전치사군입니다.

about, on, concerning, regarding, pertaining to, with regard to,
in respect of, as for, as to

03. 구전치사(Compound Prepositions) 암기 리스트

  • in addition to (~외에 더하여)
  • in place of / instead of (~대신에)
  • in celebration of (~을 축하하여)
  • in honor of (~에 경의를 표하며)
  • in response to (~에 응하여)
  • in preparation for (~에 대비하여)
  • on behalf of (~를 대표하여/대신하여)
  • regardless of (~에 상관없이)

Day 10: Actual Check-up (Preposition Mastery)

01. Free admission tickets _______ the new modern arts museum were distributed to the first two hundred visitors yesterday morning.
(A) in   (B) at   (C) on   (D) to
02. Corporate profits have increased steadily ________ the past ten years due to aggressive expansion into Asian markets.
(A) since   (B) on   (C) over   (D) about
03. The annual shareholders' meeting is scheduled to take place _______ the second floor of the company's headquarters.
(A) in   (B) at   (C) on   (D) to
04. All department monthly reports must be submitted to the regional manager _______ the end of business on Friday.
(A) by   (B) until   (C) since   (D) during
05. The technical team worked _______ the night to ensure that the server maintenance was completed before the Monday commute.
(A) among   (B) between   (C) throughout   (D) along
06. The newly proposed budget for office decor was rejected _______ to the urgent need for high-quality medical equipment.
(A) because   (B) regarding   (C) due   (D) despite
