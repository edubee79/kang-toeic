Unit 01. 가정법(Conditionals)의 공식과 시제 일치

01. 가정법 핵심 출제 패턴 (The Final Logic)

가정법은 '사실과 반대'되는 일을 가정하며, If절과 주절의 시제 짝을 맞추는 것이 핵심입니다.

종류 If 절 (조건) 주절 (결과) 토익 실전 포인트
가정법 과거 If + S + 과거동사(were) S + would/could + 원형 현재 사실과 반대
(내가 너라면...)
가정법 과거완료 If + S + had p.p. S + would have p.p. 과거 사실과 반대
(그때 그랬더라면...)
가정법 미래 If + S + should + 원형 주어 + will / 원형(명령문) 희박한 가능성, 정중한 제안
명령문과의 결합 (가정법 미래):
If절에 should가 오면 주절은 Please + 동사원형 형태의 명령문이 잘 어울립니다.
Ex) Should any problems occur, please call the service center.

Unit 02. 도치 구문(Inversion)의 원리와 가정법 도치

01. 가정법 도치 (If 생략형)

If가 생략되면 조동사가 주어 앞으로 튀어 나옵니다.

  • 과거: Were I you, I would... (If I were you...)
  • 과거완료: Had I known the fact, I... (If I had known...)
  • 미래: Should you have any questions, ... (If you should have...)

02. 부정어 도치 (Negative Inversion)

강조를 위해 부정어가 문두에 올 때 의문문 어순으로 도치됩니다.

Never / Seldom / Rarely / Hardly / Scarcely / Little + do/does/did/have + S + V
Ex) Never did we expect such an increase in sales. (우리는 전혀 예상치 못했다)

03. 보어 및 장소 부사구 도치

  • 보어 도치: Enclosed is the report. (보고서가 동봉되어 있다)
  • Only 부사구 도치: Only after the completion... should the plant begin.

Unit 03. 조동사 완료형과 가정법 관용 표현

01. 조동사 + have p.p. (과거 추측/후회)

표현 의미 실전 예문
should have p.p. ~했어야 했는데 (후회) I should have checked the stock.
could have p.p. ~할 수도 있었는데 (가능) We could have won the contract.
would have p.p. ~했을 텐데 (가정) It would have been better if...
must have p.p. ~했음에 틀림없다 (확신) He must have left the office early.

02. 가정법 관련 관용 표현 리스트

  • if any (만약 있다면)
  • if so (만일 그렇다면)
  • if not (그렇지 않다면)
  • otherwise (그렇지 않으면)
  • if it were not for (~이 없다면)
  • if it had not been for (~이 없었더라면)
Ex) Please leave your comments, if any, at the bottom of the form.

Day 14. Actual Check-up (Subjunctive Mastery)

01. _______ you have any problems with our products, we will immediately send our technical repairman to fix them at no additional cost.
(A) Have   (B) Should   (C) Will have   (D) Had
02. If the user manual _______ written more carefully by the technical writers, we could have operated the equipment much more easily.
(A) was   (B) were   (C) had been   (D) has been
03. _______ have our sales representatives seen such a significant increase in demand for premium products during the winter season.
(A) Ever   (B) Although   (C) Seldom   (D) Even
04. The board of directors maintains that if the proposed plans _______ approved last week, the construction would have started by now.
(A) are   (B) were   (C) had been   (D) into been
05. _______ is the revised list of our latest products currently available in all of our retail branches across the nation.
(A) Enclosed   (B) Enclosure   (C) Enclosing   (D) Enclose
06. The supervisor requested that staff members leave their specific comments, if _______, on the server maintenance report.
(A) so   (B) any   (C) not   (D) case
[정답 및 가이드]
