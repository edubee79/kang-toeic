Unit 01. 비교 구문의 기본과 출제 공식

01. 비교(Comparison) 핵심 출제 패턴

① 원급 비교: as + 형용사/부사 + as
Confidence is as significant as experience in the workplace.
② 비교급 비교: 비교급 + than
The new line is more efficient than those of its rivals.
③ 최상급 비교: the + 최상급 + of/in/that절
City Hall is the oldest building of all the structures.
④ 품사 결정: as~as 나 비교급 앞의 동사를 보고 결정
I drove more slowly (slow X) than usual. (동사 drove 수식)

02. 비교급/최상급 강조 부사 (Adverbs of Emphasis)

원급을 꾸미는 부사와 비교급을 꾸미는 부사는 절대 섞일 수 없습니다.

원급 강조 (Very류) 비교급 강조 (Much류 - "훨씬")
very, quite, too, so much, even, still, far, a lot, by far
최신 기출: noticeably, considerably, significantly, substantially
최상급 강조: by far, quite, much는 the 앞에 / very, single은 the 뒤에 위치합니다.

Unit 02. 비교 구문 필수 암기 표현 및 라틴어 비교

01. Than 대신 To를 쓰는 비교 (라틴어 비교급)

  • prior to (~보다 이전에 = before)
  • superior to (~보다 우수한) / inferior to (~보다 열등한)
  • prefer A to B (B보다 A를 선호하다)

02. [핵심] 비교 관련 관용 표현

표현 해석 및 출제 포인트
as ~ as possible 가능한 한 가장 ~하게 (= as ~ as S can)
no later than 늦어도 ~까지 (시점 전치사 by와 유사)
no sooner than ~하자마자 (= as soon as)
rather than ~라기 보다는 / ~하는 것보다 차라리
more than doubled 두 배 이상이 되었다 (동사로 쓰임)
at the latest 아무리 늦어도 (문장 맨 뒤 위치)
더 ~할수록 더 ~하다:
The more you exercise, the healthier you will be.
(The + 비교급 ..., The + 비교급 ... 구문은 동사 생략 가능)

Unit 03. 병치(Parallelism)와 도치 도식화

01. 등위/상관 접속사의 병치 원칙

and, but, or 등으로 연결되는 요소는 품사, 형태, 구조가 일치해야 합니다.

  • 단어 병렬: Staff members and executive managers (명사+명사)
  • 구 병렬: to find errors and to correct them (to부정사+to부정사)
  • 형용사 병렬: more detailed but more practical

02. 토익 빈출 도치(Inversion) 구문 복습

도치 원인 어순 및 예문
부정어 문두 Never / Seldom / Rarely + Do/Have + S + V
보어 문두 Enclosed / Attached + Be동사 + 주어
Only 부사구 Only after ... + Should/Can + S + V
Ex) Attached are the results of the employee survey.

Day 15. Actual Check-up (Final Comparison)

01. Certainly, Mr. Henny will be promoted to senior section manager because he is the _______ staff member in the entire regional division.
(A) dependable   (B) most dependable   (C) most dependent   (D) more dependently
02. The marketing team is currently seeking an editor with previous experience _______ excellent professional communication skills.
(A) such   (B) but   (C) and   (D) unless
03. The mission of this community health clinic is to provide as _______ much information as possible on vital public health issues.
(A) as   (B) more   (C) than   (D) very
04. Payment of monthly parking vouchers can be made either by personal check _______ by automatic withdrawal from a registered bank account.
(A) but   (B) and   (C) or   (D) if
05. The advertising campaign for the newly launched Cool Fizz drink will feature fruit flavor _______ price in its upcoming television commercial.
(A) rather than   (B) in the event of   (C) except for   (D) as for
06. Recently, the manufacturing costs of new cars have risen _____ high that most consumers have decided to maintain their current used vehicles.
(A) too   (B) such   (C) very   (D) so
[정답 및 가이드]
1.(B) 최상급(the + ...est). | 2.(C) experience와 skills를 연결하는 명사 병렬. | 3.(A) as much ~ as possible (원급 비교 관용구). | 4.(C) either A or B (상관접속사 짝궁). | 5.(A) rather than (~라기보다). | 6.(D) so ~ that 구문.
